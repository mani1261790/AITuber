# Tutor VRM model

`tutor.vrm` is the "Seed-san" model authored by VirtualCast, Inc. and published
as an official VRM specification sample:
https://github.com/vrm-c/vrm-specification/tree/master/samples/Seed-san

The model retains its own VRM metadata and is not covered by this repository's
software license. Its embedded VRM 1.0 metadata permits use by everyone,
corporate commercial use, redistribution, and redistribution after modification
under the VRM Public License 1.0: https://vrm.dev/licenses/1.0/

Credit is required by the embedded metadata: `Seed-san · VirtualCast, Inc.`
The application includes this credit in the character's accessible description.
The optional robotic backpack arm is hidden in the classroom presentation.
